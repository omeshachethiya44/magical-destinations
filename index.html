THE STORY BEHIND THE JOURNEY

Meet the Founder

Kasun Kalhara Godage (Kass)

“Travel is not just about where you go; it’s about how the journey makes you feel.”

Happy travels,
Kass
Founder, Magical Destinations

I’m Kass, a Sri Lankan traveller from the beautiful southern coast of Sri Lanka, living a simple life with a deep love for nature and exploration. To me, nature is the greatest luxury, and travel is a journey back to ourselves. It allows me to discover diverse cultures, connect with people, taste new cuisines, and experience the extraordinary beauty of our world with my heart and soul.

For me, travelling is more than visiting places; it is a way to find balance, step away from everyday stress, reconnect with myself, and appreciate the beauty of being alive. When I travel, I feel truly present, free, and connected to the world around me.

This passion inspired me to create Magical Destinations — a company built on the belief that everyone deserves the opportunity to explore the world, discover new perspectives, and create meaningful memories. My vision is to make travel more accessible, personal, and enjoyable, helping people experience the world beyond their everyday routines.

Through Magical Destinations, I hope to inspire more people to travel with an open heart, embrace different cultures, reconnect with nature, and find their own moments of happiness and inner peace.
